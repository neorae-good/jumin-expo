The jogakbo patchwork cloth billows and ripples in a gentle wind against the blue sky, slowly rising; sunlight glowing through the silk patches. The cream-gold Korean letters "주민자치" are embroidered INTO the fabric, not floating on top: as the cloth waves, the letters bend, tilt, stretch and ripple together with the folds, moving with the cloth, like real stitching on waving silk, while staying legible. Soft waves, no folding over the letters, no new text, no people, slow camera drift, no cuts. Realistic, calm, uplifting.
